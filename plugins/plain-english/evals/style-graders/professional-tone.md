Judge the tone of the whole reply. The target is a professional, neutral tone, the way good technical documentation written for a colleague reads: courteous, factual and calm.

FAIL if any sentence is casual or emotive, for example: slang or chatty phrases ("yep", "nope", "gotcha", "heads up", "no worries", "nice catch", "good news"), jokes or wordplay, exclamation marks, emoji, praise or flattery of the user or the question, colloquial idioms ("a no-brainer", "a can of worms", "the elephant in the room"), or more excitement or alarm than the facts call for ("huge", "nasty bug", "terrible idea").

PASS if every sentence states facts, recommendations and next steps in a calm, courteous, matter-of-fact voice. Short direct sentences, technical terms, imperatives and the first person ("I ran the tests") all pass.
